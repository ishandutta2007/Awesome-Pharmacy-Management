# Awesome-Pharmacy-Management

# Top Pharmacy Management Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Pharmacy Management Systems (PMS), Dispensing, Inventory, Workflow & Independent Pharmacy Software*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Pharmacy Management**. These tools support prescription processing, dispensing, inventory control, claims adjudication, patient profiles, workflow management, and overall operations for independent, retail, and specialty pharmacies.

**Examples** include PioneerRx, PrimeRx, Liberty Software, BestRx, Datascan, Computer-Rx, Winpharm, QS/1, Rx30, and ScriptPro (the category leaders in the independent and retail pharmacy market).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting pharmacy inventory, POS, dispensing, e-prescribing, and management systems — ideal for developers, clinics, free pharmacies, and organizations seeking transparent, customizable alternatives (note: full regulatory-compliant commercial PMS equivalents remain limited in pure open source).

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[PioneerRx](https://www.pioneerrx.com/)** (RedSail Technologies)  
  Feature-rich pharmacy management system widely regarded as a market leader for independent pharmacies, offering advanced dispensing, clinical tools, workflow customization, inventory, and patient care features.

- **[PrimeRx](https://www.primerx.com/)** (Micro Merchant Systems)  
  Comprehensive pharmacy management platform covering prescription intake, dispensing, inventory, billing, patient engagement, and support for retail, specialty, LTC, and compounding workflows.

- **[Liberty Software](https://www.libertysoftware.com/)**  
  Pharmacy management solution focused on independent pharmacies with strong dispensing, inventory, and operational tools.

- **[BestRx](https://www.bestrx.com/)**  
  Affordable and capable pharmacy management software popular with smaller independent pharmacies for core dispensing and workflow needs.

- **[Datascan / WinPharm](https://www.datascan.com/)**  
  Long-standing pharmacy management and POS solutions used by independent pharmacies for dispensing and front-end operations.

- **[Computer-Rx](https://www.computer-rx.com/)**  
  Pharmacy management system providing prescription processing, inventory, and business tools for retail pharmacies.

- **[QS/1 (NRx)](https://www.qs1.com/)**  
  Established pharmacy management platform (now part of broader RedSail offerings) supporting dispensing, claims, and pharmacy operations.

- **[Rx30](https://www.rx30.com/)**  
  Pharmacy management software focused on independent pharmacies with dispensing, inventory, and workflow capabilities.

- **[ScriptPro](https://www.scriptpro.com/)**  
  Pharmacy automation and management solutions combining robotics with software for high-volume and efficient dispensing operations.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** (+ Healthcare / Pharmacy modules)  
  Powerful open-source ERP that can be extended for pharmacy inventory, POS, purchasing, batch/expiry tracking, and basic dispensing workflows; one of the most mature foundations for custom pharmacy systems.

- **[OpenBoxes](https://openboxes.com/)** [](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)  
  Open-source inventory and supply-chain management system built for healthcare settings, with strong support for lot tracking, expiry alerts, multi-facility stock, and pharmacy/warehouse use cases.

- **[OPENeP](https://github.com/AppertaFoundation/openep)**  
  Open-source electronic prescribing and medication management system covering reconciliation, prescribing, pharmacist review, and administration — useful as a complementary component to pharmacy operations.

- **[OpenMRS Dispensing](https://github.com/openmrs/openmrs-esm-dispensing-app)** (+ related modules)  
  Open-source medication dispensing functionality within the OpenMRS ecosystem, suitable for clinic and hospital pharmacy workflows integrated with electronic medical records.

- **[PharmaOS](https://github.com/Skillyme-Cohort-1/pharmaOS)** and similar full-stack projects  
  Modern open-source pharmacy internal management systems covering inventory, expiry tracking, POS/sales, orders, customers, suppliers, and analytics.

- **[Various Pharmacy Management Systems](https://github.com/SammyOngaya/Pharmacy-Management-System)** (and community forks)  
  Open-source POS and inventory solutions designed for small-to-medium pharmacies and chemists, including sales, stock control, and basic reporting.

- **[PharmaHub / DRXStore / Elyxir-style projects](https://github.com/kanhaiya-gupta/PharmaHub)**  
  Community-built open-source pharmacy and medical-store management systems with multi-store support, inventory, sales, purchase tracking, and dashboards.

- **[Open-source e-prescribing & drug-order modules](https://github.com/HariniParth/OpenMRS-DrugOrders-Pharmacy)**  
  Modules and systems focused on drug orders, pharmacy review, and e-prescription workflows that can integrate with broader pharmacy management stacks.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Odoo Community** with pharmacy/inventory/POS modules for customizable retail pharmacy operations.
- **Simple PHP/Laravel/Node/React pharmacy POS and inventory systems** frequently published on GitHub for small pharmacies and learning projects.
- **Batch/expiry and inventory-focused tools** that emphasize FEFO, alerts, and multi-location stock control.
- **Integration layers** between open EMR systems (OpenMRS, OpenEMR) and custom dispensing front-ends.
- Many academic and clinic-oriented projects for free-clinic or low-resource pharmacy dispensing and inventory control.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **ERPNext or Odoo** for core inventory/accounting/POS, **OpenBoxes** for healthcare-grade stock and expiry management, and **OpenMRS/OPENeP components** for prescribing and clinical medication workflows to create a tailored, self-hosted pharmacy management stack.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Pharmacy management systems involve regulated activities (dispensing, controlled substances, claims, patient data). Any self-hosted or open-source solution must be carefully validated for local legal, regulatory (e.g., DEA, state boards, HIPAA), and clinical safety requirements before production use.
- Commercial pharmacy software often includes certified interfaces for claims, e-prescribing, and automation that pure open-source projects may lack.
